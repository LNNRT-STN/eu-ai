# Transport infrastructure in the July 2021 flood: what happened

Companion to `outputs/data/infra_in_flood.csv` (built by `notebooks/infra.py`). The CSV says which of today's
railways, stations, bridges and main roads lie inside the 2021 flood extent; this page says what operators and
governments reported about the damage. All figures are quoted from the linked source; nothing here is estimated by us.

## What our map shows
From OSM 2026 and the all-passes satellite flood (Vesdre: EFAS 500-year model). Ahr: 13.7 km of the Ahr valley line
(DB 3000) in the water, 6 stations/halts (Walporzheim, Heimersheim/Lohrsdorf, Dernau, Mayschoß, Altenahr, Ahrbrück),
17 rail bridges on it (19 with the Rhine line at Sinzig). Schleiden: 2.5 km of the Eifel line (DB 2631) plus sidings around Kall, stations Kall, Gemünd, Olef.
Euskirchen: 0.3 km of the Erft valley line, halt Bad Münstereifel-Arloff, 2 rail bridges. Vesdre (*modelled*): 23.1 km
of line 37, plus L44, L43 and HSL 3; stations Chênée, Chaudfontaine, Juslenville; 38 rail bridges. Other areas: under
1 km of rail each. The Ahr result fits DB's own count (22 bridges, 10 damaged stations), which supports the method.

## Germany (DB InfraGO)
- **Ahr valley line (Ahrtalbahn, Remagen–Ahrbrück).** DB, 12 Dec 2025: the "29 Kilometer langen, in Teilen komplett
  von den Wassermassen zerstörten Strecke" reopens to passengers on **14 December 2025**; rebuilt: "18 Kilometer
  Gleise, 22 Brücken, fünf Tunnel sowie zehn beschädigte Stationen" (15 bridges new, 7 renovated);
  "Das Investitionsvolumen betrug insgesamt rund **590 Millionen Euro**", financed by the federal government.
  So the upper line was closed for about **4 years and 5 months**.
  [DB press release](https://www.deutschebahn.com/de/presse/pressestart_zentrales_uebersicht/Die-Bahn-ist-zurueck-im-Ahrtal--13699724)
  *Not verified from a primary source:* the date the lower section Remagen–Walporzheim reopened (end of 2021 per
  secondary sources).
- **Eifel line (Eifelstrecke, Hürth-Kalscheuren–Euskirchen–Kall–Gerolstein–Trier).** Federal government answer
  (Oct 2021): Hürth-Kalscheuren–Euskirchen back "am 6. September 2021"; the rest planned in stages to summer 2023
  ([Bundestag hib 1064/2021](https://www.bundestag.de/presse/hib/kurzmeldungen-865884)). Federal transport ministry,
  16 Jun 2025: "Der Abschnitt zwischen Gerolstein & Kall ist endlich wieder befahrbar!"; "20 Brücken, zahlreiche
  Stationen und 10 Kilometer Bahndamm wurden hierfür erneuert"
  ([BMV](https://www.bmv.de/SharedDocs/DE/Artikel/K/zuege-fahren-wieder-gerolstein-koeln.html)). Through service was
  thus restored almost **4 years** after the flood, two years later than first planned. Cost: *not verified*.
- **Erft valley line (Erfttalbahn, S 23 Euskirchen–Bad Münstereifel).** DB, 15 Dec 2025: back "zum Fahrplanwechsel
  am 14. Dezember"; rebuilt "auf rund 13 Kilometern Bahnstrecke", "zwei Brücken wieder aufgebaut, eine Brücke
  saniert". Cost: *not verified*.
  [DB press release](https://www.deutschebahn.com/de/presse/presse-regional/pr-duesseldorf-de/presseinformationen-regional/Bad-Muenstereifel-S-23-faehrt-nach-der-Jahrhundertflut-wieder-auf-der-Erfttalbahn-13701696)
- **DB network total.** An early DB estimate of €1.3 billion for infrastructure damage is cited in parliamentary
  material, but in Oct 2021 the government said the damage could "noch nicht beziffert werden" (hib above).
  *Treat €1.3 bn as unverified.*

## Belgium (Infrabel)
- Infrabel, 19 Jul 2021: damage "entre 40 et 50 millions d'euros" (provisional), "une dizaine de kilomètres de voies
  ferrées" washed away, "environ 50 kilomètres de lignes aériennes", three rail bridges seriously damaged, 25 lines hit
  ([DH](https://www.dhnet.be/actu/belgique/2021/07/19/intemperies-les-dommages-au-reseau-ferroviaire-estimes-entre-40-et-50-millions-deuros-6S6XAU5Y5ZF5RPTW6U3VWRAIQM/)).
- Infrabel annual safety report 2021: "Plus de 200 km de voies réparties sur 13 lignes ferroviaires différentes ont été
  détériorées"; "En trois mois, environ **60 millions d'euros** ont dû être investi"
  ([Infrabel PDF](https://infrabel.be/sites/default/files/generated/files/report/Rapport%20annuel%20de%20se%CC%81curite%CC%81%202021%20FR.pdf)).
- **Vesdre line 37 (Liège–Verviers–Aachen).** At La Brouck ballast washed away over 50 m; at Louheau (Pepinster)
  "le pont a été détruit". Planned reopening Liège–Nessonvaux 1 Sep 2021, Liège–Pepinster/Verviers **13 Sep 2021**
  (about 2 months closed). Line 44 Pepinster–Spa: Pepinster-cité bridge "pillier central s'est effondré", target
  4 Oct 2021 ([DH, 23 Aug 2021](https://www.dhnet.be/actu/belgique/2021/08/23/six-semaines-apres-les-inondations-limpressionnante-course-contre-la-montre-dinfrabel-pour-remettre-le-train-sur-les-rails-photosvideo-YEN6S57GJ5H2VPBNTRK6EO22TM/)).
  These are planned dates as reported; the actual reopening dates are *not verified*.

## Netherlands

## Limits
OSM is the network of 2026, not 2021: rebuilt lines and bridges may have moved, been raised or been added (the Ahr
line was electrified in the rebuild). "In the flood" means the line crosses the mapped water, not that it was damaged.
Vesdre figures come from the EFAS 500-year model, not from observation.
