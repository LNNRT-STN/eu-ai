# Visual and interaction ideas for the 5-minute pitch

Desk research, 29 Oct 2026. Reference for look and feel: [FireTrace](https://firemap-two.vercel.app/). Every idea uses open data and free tiles, works with Streamlit-native controls, and never relies on colour alone. Hours are rough estimates for one coder already familiar with our `map3d` module. Anything outside the four challenge datasets (Annex 2) is marked **external** and must be labelled that way in the app.

## Ideas, ranked by impact per hour

| # | Idea | Role | Build (stack, hours) | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Scripted camera per act.** One button per story beat (valley overview, Schuld, Altenahr, Verviers) flies the 3D camera there, with a slow orbit while you talk. Ref: [MapLibre camera around a point](https://maplibre.org/maplibre-gl-js/docs/examples/animate-map-camera-around-a-point/) | All, mostly the jury | `flyTo` / `easeTo` with fixed pitch and bearing, triggered by `st.segmented_control`. **1 h** | Low. Bad tiles on venue wifi: pre-warm each view once before the pitch. |
| 2 | **Timeline bar with playback and event ticks.** A Windy-style strip under the map with ticks for "EFAS alert issued", "first EMS map" and "peak", plus a play button. Ref: [Windy](https://www.windy.com), [MapLibre time slider](https://maplibre.org/maplibre-gl-js/docs/examples/create-a-time-slider/) | Crisis room | Run playback inside the JS component and use `setFilter` on a time property. Streamlit only sends the start time and speed (`st.select_slider`). **2 h** | Streamlit reruns cause flicker if every frame goes through Python. Keep the animation loop in JS. |
| 3 | **Gauge hydrograph synced to the timeline.** Water level over time, a vertical "now" line that follows playback, and labelled threshold bands (text, not just colour). Ref: [NOAA NWPS gauge hydrograph](https://water.noaa.gov/gauges/10319900), [Hochwasservorhersage RLP](https://www.hochwasser.rlp.de) | Crisis room | Altair line chart with a `rule` mark and a text label on each band, in `st.altair_chart`. **2 h** | The series is **external** (not in Annex 2). The Altenahr gauge was washed away near the peak, so show the gap honestly as "gauge lost". |
| 4 | **Time-stepped observed flood extent.** Copernicus EMS delineations shown in order of satellite pass, each stamped with its acquisition time. Ref: [EMSR517 Germany](https://mapping.emergency.copernicus.eu/activations/EMSR517), [EMSR518 Belgium](https://mapping.emergency.copernicus.eu/activations/EMSR518/), [EMSR520 Netherlands](https://mapping.emergency.copernicus.eu/activations/EMSR520) | Crisis room | A `fill` layer draped on the terrain, filtered on the acquisition date and driven by idea 2. **2 h** | The products are snapshots, not continuous coverage. Label them "observed at hh:mm". Never interpolate between snapshots. |
| 5 | **Calm, legible valley.** Use hillshade and contour lines at low opacity over the satellite base, so the valley shape is readable without bright hazard colours. Ref: [hillshade](https://maplibre.org/maplibre-gl-js/docs/examples/add-a-hillshade-layer/), [contour lines](https://maplibre.org/maplibre-gl-js/docs/examples/add-contour-lines/) | All | `hillshade` layer from the existing DEM source. Contours via `maplibre-contour` from jsDelivr. **1 h** | Low. Contours add tile work, so drop them if the frame rate falls. |
| 6 | **Synced counters with sparklines.** Three tiles: people in observed water, homes not yet self-reported, beds offered. Each updates with playback. Ref: FireTrace side charts | Crisis room | `st.metric` with `chart_data` (sparkline) and `delta`. **1 h** | The numbers must come from GHS-POP and GHS-OBAT intersections. Never use invented figures. Show "modelled" next to population counts. |
| 7 | **Address card: "leave now, go to X".** Type an address, see the verdict in words plus an icon, and an animated route to the shelter. Ref: [overstroomik.nl](https://www.overstroomik.nl) (address → water depth → what to do), [MapLibre animate a line](https://maplibre.org/maplibre-gl-js/docs/examples/animate-a-line/) | Residents | `st.text_input` with geocoding, then a `line` layer with `line-dasharray` animation. **2–3 h** | Public geocoders and routers (Nominatim, OSRM demo) have rate limits. Precompute two demo addresses and keep them cached. |
| 8 | **Home status layer for crews.** Homes coloured and shaped by status: self-reported safe (check icon), not reported (hollow ring), offering a bed (bed icon). Ref: [MapLibre buildings in 3D](https://maplibre.org/maplibre-gl-js/docs/examples/display-buildings-in-3d/) | Fire crews | `fill-extrusion` if GHS-OBAT gives footprints (check Annex 2), otherwise a `symbol` layer with icons. **2–3 h** | Self-reports are simulated in a replay. Say so on screen ("simulated reports"). |
| 9 | **Road closures from EMS grading.** Blocked or damaged roads drawn as dashed lines with a "closed" label, so routes avoid them. Ref: EMS grading products inside [EMSR517](https://mapping.emergency.copernicus.eu/activations/EMSR517) | Fire crews, residents | `line` layer with `line-dasharray` and a `symbol` label, read from the EMS transport vectors (SHP). **1.5 h** | Not every area of interest has a grading product. Show "no assessment" rather than "open". |
| 10 | **Warning lead-time strip.** A single bar from the first EFAS notification to the local peak, with the gap in hours written out. This is the policy story in one picture. Ref: [Copernicus EMS, 16 July 2021](https://mapping.emergency.copernicus.eu/news/the-copernicus-emergency-management-service-forecasts-notifies-and-monitors-devastating-floods-in-germany-netherlands-belgium-and-switzerland/), [EFAS](https://www.efas.eu) | Jury, crisis room | Altair bar chart or the ticks from idea 2. **1 h** | The notification times are **external** (EFAS hazard maps in Annex 2 are not alerts). Cite the source on screen. |
| 11 | **Population grid in the flood zone.** GHS-POP cells inside the observed extent, drawn flat with labelled classes. Ref: [deck.gl GridCellLayer](https://deck.gl/docs/api-reference/layers/grid-cell-layer), [kepler.gl demo](https://kepler.gl/demo) | Crisis room | pydeck `GridCellLayer` with extrusion off, or a MapLibre `fill` layer. **2 h** | Tall columns look alarmist and hide the terrain, so keep them flat. GHS-POP is a residential estimate, not people present at night. |
| 12 | **Before/after swipe.** A slider swipes between pre-event and post-event imagery of one village. Ref: [DLR, flood areas in Ahrweiler](https://www.dlr.de/en/latest/news/2023/02/25-years-of-copernicus-a-success-story/flood-areas-in-the-ahrweiler-region), `maplibre-gl-compare` | Jury | Two synced maps, both with a raster source. **3 h** | Free post-event tiles are hard to find. Only build this if a georeferenced EMS image (TIFF) is already in hand. |

**Do first:** 1, 5, 6, 10 (about 4 h together), then 2 → 4 → 3, which belong together. 7 and 8 are the role demos. Leave 11 and 12 out unless there is time to spare.

**Accessibility, applies to all:** use one blue for water and one warm tone for "needs action". Pair every status with an icon or line style and a word. Aim for at least 3:1 contrast on the satellite base. Use no red-flashing alerts.

## 5-minute storyboard (10 shots, about 30 s each)

1. **Hook (0:00).** The orbiting valley overview of the Ahr in 3D, quiet. Voice-over: "14 July 2021, 21:00. The warnings existed." (ideas 1, 5)
2. **Lead time (0:30).** The warning lead-time strip: hours between the first EFAS notification and the peak. Name the public actor the tool is for. (10)
3. **Press play (1:00).** The timeline runs. EMS extents appear pass by pass, each with its timestamp. (2, 4)
4. **Synced evidence (1:30).** The hydrograph's "now" line climbs through the threshold bands. The counters move with it. Pause at "gauge lost". (3, 6)
5. **Crisis room view (2:00).** Fly to Schuld. Population cells inside the water, labelled "modelled". One sentence on what the crisis room decides here. (1, 6)
6. **Resident (2:30).** Switch role. Type a demo address. The card reads "Leave now, go to [shelter]" and the route animates. (7)
7. **Second resident (3:00).** Another address: "You are safe. Can you offer a bed?" One click, and the beds counter goes up. (7, 6)
8. **Fire crew (3:30).** Switch role. Homes not yet reported appear as hollow rings, closed roads as dashed lines. The crew list sorts by distance. (8, 9)
9. **Cross-border (4:00).** Fly to Verviers on the Vesdre: same tool, same legend, Belgian data. (1, 4)
10. **Close (4:30).** Back to the overview. Name the data limits on screen (snapshots, modelled population, simulated reports), state the ask, end on the lead-time number. (1, 10)
