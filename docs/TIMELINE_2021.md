# July 2021 flood: official warning and evacuation timeline (sourced)

**Headline comparison candidates (2021 side; Y is whatever the replay says):**
1. **Ahr:** EFAS sent its first Rhine notification on Sat 10 July (11:35 CEST), and flash-flood notifications covering the Ahr region (Koblenz) followed on Tue 13 July at 11:25 CEST. Kreis Ahrweiler's evacuation call for 50 m either side of the Ahr went out only at **23:09 on 14 July**, after the Altenahr gauge had already failed at 19:15 and 19:45.
2. **Vesdre:** The Liège province issued a general evacuation order for the Vesdre valley at **17:57 on 14 July**, but its 16:34 police order did not cover Verviers. The Eupen dam began releasing water around 18:45 and was at its storage limit by 22:47.
3. **Erft:** The DWD's extreme-rain warning (level 4 of 4) reached the Erftstadt fire brigade at 09:40 on 13 July. Blessem and Bliesheim were evacuated only after a dam-failure call at **about 08:10 on 15 July**.

All times are local (CEST = UTC+2) unless marked otherwise. "Verified" means the time and event were read in the cited document or page itself. "Reported" means they come from a secondary source, or from a primary source that could not be opened (for example a paywall). "Not found" means no reliable time was located. Times that were not found have not been estimated.

## Caveats for the pitch

- **Hindsight data.** The replay drives river flows from the **GloFAS hindcast**, a reanalysis made after the fact with observed meteorology. It does **not** use the forecasts that were available on 10–14 July 2021. A "the tool would have said evacuate at Y" claim is therefore an upper bound on what an operational system could have done. The fair comparison is against what EFAS and the LfU actually forecast at the time (sections 1 and 4): the LfU's best forecast for Altenahr was about 7 m against about 10 m observed.
- **±12 h timing.** The replay's event times are uncertain by about ±12 hours, because of daily hindcast resolution and routing. Do not set a replay time against a 2021 order time that is only a few hours earlier or later and claim a lead-time win. Only differences well beyond 12 h (for example 10 July EFAS versus the 23:09 order on 14 July) are robust.
- **Order times differ by municipality and by step.** "The evacuation order" is not a single moment. In Ahrweiler, alarm level 4 came at 17:40, level 5 at about 22:04, the evacuation recommendation at 23:09, and orders for individual streets later. In Wallonia, Eupen issued orders at 14:16, the province at 13:29 and a Vesdre-wide general order at 17:57, while Verviers was excluded at 16:34. In Limburg, Valkenburg acted at 22:13 on 14 July, Roermond at 14:39 on 15 July and Venlo at 18:00 on 16 July. Always name the municipality and the step.
- **Warnings versus orders.** EFAS notifications went to national and regional hydrological agencies, not to the districts or mayors who order evacuations. NRW received none. DWD warnings are about rainfall, not river levels. The gap between "information existed" and "an order was given" is partly institutional, and the pitch should say so rather than imply that the forecasts alone were ignored.
- **Source quality.** The Ahr times come from a prosecutor's summary (Vorlage 18/5776). The Untersuchungsausschuss final report (Drs. 18/10000) was not read directly. Several Altenahr forecast values (5.19 m, 6.81 m, 7.07 m) come only from a paywalled Rheinpfalz chronology and are marked "reported". The Altenahr peak timing conflicts: the LfU gives about 02:00 on 15 July, an eyewitness about 22:45 on 14 July.
- **Timezones.** All times are CEST. The JRC report labels the same EFAS issue times as CEST in its tables but UTC in its figure captions (see section 1).

## Tool side, as shown in the Crisis room (checked 29 Oct by the lead)

The 2021 times above were spot-checked in the primary documents. In the RLP Landtag Vorlage 18/5776: 17.40 Uhr
alarm level 4, about 22.04 Uhr level 5, 21.00 Uhr "expliziter Evakuierungsaufruf dringend geboten", 23.09 Uhr
recommendation. In the Walloon inquiry report: 16 h 34 the governor's order, 17 h 57 the "ordre général d'évacuation"
